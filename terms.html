Using this website

This website is run by Kimberrys Cafe & Bakery, Okodan Road, Osu, Accra, Ghana. By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please stop using the site.

We try to keep the site accurate and available, but we do not promise that it will always be online or free of mistakes. We may update the menu, prices, photos or these terms at any time.

Menu & prices

All prices are shown in Ghana cedis (GH₵) and are subject to change. Menu items are subject to availability — if something has sold out, we will let you know and suggest an alternative.

Photos are for illustration. The cake or dish you receive may look slightly different, especially where decorations, fruit or finishes vary with the season.

Cake prices are listed by size and number of layers. Add-ons such as fresh flowers, cake toppers, edible prints, fondant decorations and delivery are priced separately and confirmed with you before we start.

Orders & payment

You can build an order on this website, but the order is only placed when you send it to us on WhatsApp and we confirm it. The website does not take payment and does not reserve items.

We will confirm the final total, availability, pickup or delivery details and payment method in the WhatsApp conversation. An order is not accepted until we confirm it.

Please check the order before you send it — wrong quantities, missing items or an incorrect phone number can delay your food.

Celebration cakes

Celebration cakes should be preordered at least one to two days ahead. Larger cakes, complicated designs and busy periods may need more notice. We will confirm what is possible when you message us.

Cake designs are made to order. We may need to substitute a decoration or finish if an ingredient or supply is unavailable; we will tell you before confirming. Once work has started, changes or cancellations may not be possible.

Allergens & dietary needs

Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, milk, nuts, soy and other allergens, so we cannot guarantee that any item is completely free from traces. If you have an allergy or dietary requirement, please tell us before ordering so we can explain what we can safely prepare.

Pickup & delivery

Pickup is from Okodan Road, Osu. Delivery may be available for an extra fee and depends on the distance and the time of day. We will confirm the fee and timing on WhatsApp. Once an order has been handed to you or to a delivery rider, it is your responsibility.
