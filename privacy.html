Who we are

Kimberrys Cafe & Bakery is a cafe and bakery on Okodan Road, Osu, Accra, Ghana. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “Kimberrys” mean Kimberrys Cafe & Bakery. You can reach us on 024 150 2882.

What we collect

We keep things simple. This website does not have a sign-up form, a payment page or a customer account.

  • Order details you choose to send. If you build an order, the dishes, quantities, name and notes stay in your own browser until you clear them or send the order to us on WhatsApp. WhatsApp then handles the message under its own privacy policy.
  • Browser storage. Your order draft and your cookie-notice choice are saved in your browser’s local storage so they are still there if you refresh the page.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider may record standard server information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Messages you send us. If you call, message us on WhatsApp or send us a note on Instagram, we receive the information you choose to share.

We do not ask for payment details on this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Cookies & storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The site currently sets no analytics or marketing cookies. We use local storage for two essential things:

  • kimberrys-order-v1 — remembers the dishes in your order while you browse.
  • kimberrys-cookie-notice-v1 — remembers that you have seen the cookie notice.

You can clear these at any time through your browser settings. Clearing the order storage simply empties your order. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for your permission first.

How we use it

  • To prepare and confirm the order you send us.
  • To answer questions about the menu, cakes, delivery or your order.
  • To keep the website secure, available and working properly.
  • To understand which parts of the menu are useful, but only if you have agreed to analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services needed to run the site and take your order:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you choose to send an order or message us.
  • Instagram (Meta) — if you open our Instagram page or message us there.
  • Google Maps — if you tap “Get directions”.
  • Our hosting provider — which stores and serves the website and its security logs.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our customers, staff or business.

How long we keep it

Your order draft stays in your browser until you clear it. WhatsApp messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your order and any follow-up, then deleted or archived according to WhatsApp’s own settings. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period. We do not keep a copy of your order on this website.

Your rights

Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) gives you rights over personal information we hold about you, including the right to ask what we hold, to have it corrected, and to ask us to stop using it. To make a request, contact us on 024 150 2882. We may need to confirm your identity before we can help.

If you are not happy with our response, you can contact Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use HTTPS across the site and keep the information we hold to the minimum needed to serve you. No website or messaging service can be completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information such as payment details in an order message.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check back if you would like to see the latest version.
